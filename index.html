    About Nexus Expense

    Nexus Expense is a simple budget tracker that runs in your web browser. Add each income or expense with a short description, an amount and a category, and the page instantly updates your total balance, total income, total expenses and a breakdown of spending by category. It is built for everyday money: groceries, fuel, bills, rent, transport, health, education and salary.

    How to use it

    1. Choose your currency symbol above the totals.
    2. Enter a description and an amount, choose Expense or Income and pick a category.
    3. Click Add transaction. The totals and category cards update immediately.
    4. Review your category totals to see where your money goes, and delete entries made by mistake.
    5. Click Export CSV to save a copy you can open in a spreadsheet.

    Where your data is stored

    Everything you enter is saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. Nexus Expense has no account system and no server database for your entries, so nobody else can see them. The trade-off is that the data lives only in that browser. If you clear your browsing data, use private mode or switch to another device, your entries will not appear there. Export a CSV from time to time as a backup. Read the full privacy policy.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is my financial data uploaded anywhere?

    No. Transactions are saved only in your browser's local storage on your own device. Nexus Expense has no server database for your entries.

    Will I lose my data if I clear my browser?

    Yes. Clearing site data, using private browsing or switching browsers or devices means your saved entries will not be there. Use the Export CSV button to keep a backup.

    Can I use it on my phone and my computer?

    The tracker works on both, but each browser keeps its own separate data. Entries do not sync between devices.

    Can I change the currency?

    Yes. Pick a currency symbol from the menu above the totals. It changes how amounts are displayed and is remembered in your browser.

    Is it really free?

    Yes. There is no account, subscription or paywall.

    Nexus Expense is a record-keeping tool and does not give financial advice.
