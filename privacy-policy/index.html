Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

1. Your transactions stay on your device

Nexus Expense saves the entries you add, along with your chosen currency, in your browser's local storage. We do not receive or store them on any server, and we cannot see them.

2. Your control

You can delete all saved entries at any time with the Clear all data button, or by clearing your browser's site data. Deleted data cannot be recovered by us. Exporting a CSV creates a file on your device only.

3. Server logs

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and operations.

4. Third-party resources

Pages load icon files from cdnjs.cloudflare.com. Your browser contacts that provider to download them, so it can see your IP address. It does not receive your transactions.

5. Contact form

If you use the contact form, your name, email address and message are sent through Formspree to us. We use them only to reply.

6. Cookies

We do not set advertising or tracking cookies.

7. Questions

Use our contact page.
