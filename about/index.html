About Nexus Expense

Nexus Expense is a free budget and expense tracker from Nexus Global Information Technology. We built it for people who want a quick, no-fuss way to see where their money goes without handing their financial details to a third party.

Private by design

Your transactions are saved only in your own browser. There is no account to create and no server database holding your entries.

Simple on purpose

You add a description, amount, type and category. The tool shows your balance and spending by category, and lets you export a CSV. We would rather do a few things well than overwhelm you.
